## What Makes Us Different From The Competition

**vs. Google reCAPTCHA / Cloudflare Turnstile:**
These tools ask users to solve puzzles to prove humanity. Proof of Human works *silently in the background* — no friction for real users, no puzzles, no interruptions. It's detection, not verification.

**vs. DataDome / PerimeterX / Imperva:**
These are enterprise-grade, expensive, and complex to set up. Proof of Human is self-serve — live in 2 minutes, free tier available, no sales call required. Built for businesses that want real-time visibility without a 6-figure contract.

**vs. Bot filtering in GA4:**
GA4's built-in bot filtering is coarse and reactive. Proof of Human analyzes behavioral signals per session and gives you a continuous confidence score — not a binary known-bot flag against a fixed list.

**vs. IPQualityScore / FraudScore:**
IP-based fraud tools tell you about the *source* of traffic. Proof of Human tells you about the *behavior* of the visitor — which is far harder to fake and far more accurate for real-time detection.

**The Proof of Human difference:**
→ Behavioral analysis, not IP blocklists
→ Real-time per-session scores (not daily batch reports)
→ Full analytics suite built in — not just a block/allow gate
→ Zero infrastructure — fully hosted, no servers to manage
→ Transparent scoring — you see *why* a session scored the way it did
→ Accessible pricing — free tier, no contracts, no sales calls
